## Discussion between NoWord and Mr G

### Does *Khatam an-Nabiyyin* mean prophethood has ended? My notes

---

### 1. The Prophet called the returning Jesus *Nabiyyullah*

In Muslim 2937a he describes what happens when the Messiah returns, and in the Arabic he says *Nabiyyullah*, the Prophet of Allah. So he used the word *nabi* for someone who appears after his death.

**Mr G's question to NoWord:** do we agree that whoever returns, in your case Jesus (AS), will be a prophet?

**NoWord's reply:** Jesus was appointed before Muhammad (PBUH). Thus this is an invalid statement to prove that a prophet can come after Muhammad.

**Mr G's follow-up answer:** Quran 5:117 (*fa-lammā tawaffaytanī*) and 3:55 tell me Jesus died, so the Messiah who comes is from within the ummah. Bukhari 3449 says "your imam will be from among you".

### 2. How the Prophet (PBUH) used a seal

In his time a seal (*khātam*) was what made a letter accepted. Bukhari 7162 says rulers wouldn't read a letter unless it was sealed, and al-Bukhari put it in a chapter on bearing witness to a sealed letter. Bukhari 5877 and Muslim 2092a say nobody else could copy his inscription. Bukhari 3106, 5873 and 5879 say Abu Bakr, ʿUmar and ʿUthman sealed with his ring after him. No new seal was made; everything went through his.
