# High-protein vegetarian meal plan (~130 g protein a day)

This is a sample day. Swap meals freely using the options below each one.

## Breakfast: about 25–30 g

- 3-egg omelette or bhurji (onion, tomato, chilli, spinach) with 1 slice of wholegrain toast
- *Swap:* 200 g Greek yoghurt or skyr, 40 g oats, berries and 1 tbsp chia or pumpkin seeds
- *Swap:* 2 moong dal chillas stuffed with 50 g paneer

## Mid-morning: about 30 g

- Whey protein shake made with 300 ml milk
- *Swap:* 200 g cottage cheese with fruit

## Lunch: about 30 g

- 1 bowl of dal, 100 g paneer or tofu bhurji, 1–2 wholemeal rotis and a salad
- *Swap:* egg curry (2–3 eggs) with 1 roti or a small portion of rice and a salad
- *Swap:* chana masala made with a full cup of chickpeas, with quinoa

## Afternoon snack: about 10–15 g

- 2 boiled eggs, or 40 g roasted chana, or 100 g edamame
- *Swap:* 2 tbsp peanut butter on wholegrain toast

## Dinner: about 30 g

- Soya chunk curry (50 g dry chunks), with rice or quinoa and vegetables
- *Swap:* tofu or tempeh stir-fry (150 g), or rajma with a side of Greek yoghurt raita
- *Swap:* veg fried rice or quinoa with 3 scrambled eggs mixed in

## Your best protein sources

| Food | Protein |
|---|---|
| Soya chunks, 50 g dry | ~25 g |
| Whey, 1 scoop | ~24 g |
| Greek yoghurt or skyr, 200 g | ~20 g |
| 3 large eggs | ~19 g |
| Tempeh, 100 g | ~19 g |
| Paneer, 100 g | ~18 g |
| Tofu, 100 g | ~12–15 g |
| Lentils or chickpeas, 1 cooked cup | ~15–18 g |

## Tips

- Eggs are cheap, quick and one of the best-quality proteins you can eat. Boil a batch at the start of the week for easy snacks.
- For fat loss, use 2 whole eggs plus 2–3 extra egg whites. Each white adds about 3.5 g of protein for very few calories.
- Paneer is high in fat and calories, so lean more on eggs, tofu, soya, Greek yoghurt and lentils.
- Keep rice and rotis moderate. Fill the rest of your plate with vegetables.
- In the UK, check the yoghurt label for about 9–10 g of protein per 100 g. Many "Greek-style" yoghurts have much less.
- Most UK whey is vegetarian, but check the label.
- Eggs and dairy give you some B12. A vitamin D supplement over winter is still worth it.

## Protein target

The 130 g target assumes a bodyweight of around 80 kg. Aim for roughly 1.6 g of protein per kg of your own bodyweight.
