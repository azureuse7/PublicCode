# Agreement

This is an agreement between Mr. S and Mr. G.

Date: 2026-10-24

## 1. Kids' food is exempted

No expense on the kids' food is to be shared between the two parties. This applies in every case:

- the parents ask for the food
- the kids ask for the food
- the host offers the food themselves

## 2. Medicine is not exempt

The expense of a child's medicine is not exempted, and the other party (whose child it is) should pay for it. This applies in every case:

- the child needs the medicine
- the child says he needs it
- the circumstances call for it
- the parents tell the other person to bring the medicine
